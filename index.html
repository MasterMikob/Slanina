    Reference notes

    Cold smoking

    • Keep smoke below 25°C, target 15–20°C. Above 25°C the fat renders and you're in the bacterial danger zone.
    • Wood: beech, oak, apple, cherry. Never pine or eucalyptus.

    Signs of spoilage

    • Discard the batch if it smells sour or ammoniac, is slimy, or grows fuzzy black/green mould.
    • A fine white bloom is normal.

    Curing salt safety

    • Curing salt is toxic in quantity. Weigh it, never estimate.
